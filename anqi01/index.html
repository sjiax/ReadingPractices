Making Resolutions

把决心坚持下去

Unit 6 · Section B 1b 原文 247 词 4 段 · 25 句 31 个生词 17 个短语 5 道题

讲“决心”是什么、常见哪几类，以及大多数人为什么坚持不下来，最后给出守住决心的办法：定可实现的目标、拆成小目标、写下来、让家人朋友提醒你。全文 25 句、4 段，适合练“找原因 + 找建议”。原卷只有“选句填空”，本页 5 道理解题为依据原文自拟，仅供参考。

语速

一原文先通读一遍，再做题

Do you know what a resolution is? A resolution is a promise that you make to yourself. People make resolutions to improve their life. The most common kind is a New Year's resolution. After all, the start of the year is often a perfect time for making resolutions.

Resolutions can be about anything. Some are about physical health. For example, some people may promise to exercise more or eat more healthily. Some resolutions have to do with learning new things. People might decide they are going to take up a hobby, like painting or photography. And some resolutions have to do with self-improvement, like becoming more confident or organized. For example, a student may decide to start a new study plan.

Although there are differences, most resolutions have one thing in common: People hardly ever keep them! But there are good reasons for this. Sometimes the resolutions may be too difficult to keep. And sometimes people just forget about them.

To make your resolutions work, you need to plan them wisely. Always make sure your resolution is possible to achieve. Don't set a goal that is too difficult or too much for you. Try to set mini-goals to help you achieve your resolution. And if you don't want to forget your resolutions, write them down. Also, talk about them with your family and friends. Encourage them to remind you of your goals. So what resolutions are you going to make? How are you going to keep them?

二阅读理解题先自己做，再展开答案

1.What is a resolution according to the passage?

根据文章，决心是什么？

  • A.A plan for a trip（一份旅行计划）
  • B.A promise you make to yourself（你对自己许下的承诺）
  • C.A kind of hobby（一种爱好）
  • D.A way to exercise（一种锻炼方式）
看答案与依据

答案：B

依据句：A resolution is a promise that you make to yourself.

解析：细节题：第一段第二句直接给定义，B 是原句的同义复述。

2.Why do most people fail to keep their resolutions?

为什么大多数人坚持不了自己的决心？

  • A.The resolutions are too hard or they forget them（决心太难，或者他们忘了）
  • B.They have no time at all（他们完全没有时间）
  • C.They do not like setting goals（他们不喜欢设定目标）
  • D.Other people stop them（别人阻止他们）
看答案与依据

答案：A

依据句：Sometimes the resolutions may be too difficult to keep. And sometimes people just forget about them.

解析：原因题：第三段连续两句给出两个原因——太难（too difficult）和忘记（forget），A 把两条合起来。

3.What does the word "mini-goals" mean in the passage?

文中 mini-goals 这个词是什么意思？

  • A.Very big goals for the future（未来的大目标）
  • B.Small goals that help you reach your resolution（帮你实现决心的小目标）
  • C.Goals for other people（给别人定的目标）
  • D.Goals that you forget easily（容易忘记的目标）
看答案与依据

答案：B

依据句：Try to set mini-goals to help you achieve your resolution.

解析：猜词题：mini-（小）+ goals（目标），且后半句 to help you achieve your resolution 说明了作用。

4.Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a kind of resolution?

下面哪一项不是文中提到的决心类型？

  • A.Physical health（身体健康）
  • B.Learning new things（学习新东西）
  • C.Making more money（挣更多的钱）
  • D.Self-improvement（自我提升）
看答案与依据

答案：C

依据句：Some are about physical health. / Some resolutions have to do with learning new things. / And some resolutions have to do with self-improvement, like becoming more confident or organized.

解析：细节题（否定问法）：第二段只提到 health / learning new things / self-improvement 三类，挣钱没提。

5.What is the passage mainly about?

这篇文章主要讲什么？

  • A.What resolutions are, why people fail, and how to keep them（决心是什么、为什么坚持不了、怎样坚持）
  • B.How to exercise every day（怎样每天锻炼）
  • C.Why hobbies are popular（为什么爱好很受欢迎）
  • D.How to write a study plan（怎样写学习计划）
看答案与依据

答案：A

依据句：To make your resolutions work, you need to plan them wisely.

解析：主旨题：全文先定义决心、再讲常见类型与失败原因，最后一段给坚持的办法；B、C、D 都只是文中一个例子。

Do you know what a resolution is?

A resolution is a promise that you make to yourself.
决心是你对自己许下的承诺。

人们下决心是为了改善自己的生活。

最常见的一种就是新年决心。

毕竟，一年之初往往是下决心的绝佳时机。

第 3 段
Although there are differences, most resolutions have one thing in common: People hardly ever keep them!
尽管决心各有不同，但大多数决心有一个共同点：人们几乎从来坚持不下来！
in common 共同，共有 · We have a lot in common. （我们有很多共同点。）
hardly ever 几乎从不 · She hardly ever watches TV. （她几乎从不看电视。）
But there are good reasons for this.
不过这也是有原因的。
Sometimes the resolutions may be too difficult to keep.
有时候决心可能太难坚持。
too … to … 太……而不能…… · The box is too heavy to carry. （这个箱子太重了，搬不动。）
And sometimes people just forget about them.
有时候人们干脆就忘了。
第 4 段
To make your resolutions work, you need to plan them wisely.
要让决心真正起作用，你需要明智地做规划。
plan sth wisely 明智地规划某事 · You should plan your time wisely. （你应该明智地规划时间。）
Always make sure your resolution is possible to achieve.
一定要确保你的决心是可能实现的。
make sure 确保，核实 · Make sure the door is closed. （确认门关好了。）
be possible to achieve 是可以实现的 · This goal is possible to achieve. （这个目标是可以实现的。）
Don't set a goal that is too difficult or too much for you.
不要给自己定太难、太重的目标。
set a goal 设定目标 · Set a goal for this month. （给这个月定一个目标。）
Try to set mini-goals to help you achieve your resolution.
试着设定一些小目标，帮助你实现自己的决心。
And if you don't want to forget your resolutions, write them down.
如果你不想忘记自己的决心，就把它们写下来。
write down 写下来 · Write down your ideas. （把你的想法写下来。）
Also, talk about them with your family and friends.
也可以和家人朋友聊聊这些决心。
talk about sth with sb 和某人谈论某事 · Talk about it with your parents. （和你父母谈谈这件事。）
Encourage them to remind you of your goals.
鼓励他们提醒你记得自己的目标。
remind sb of sth 使某人想起某事 · This song reminds me of my school. （这首歌让我想起我的学校。）
encourage sb to do sth 鼓励某人做某事 · Teachers encourage us to read. （老师鼓励我们读书。） 用法：encourage 后面接 to do，不接 doing。
So what resolutions are you going to make?
那么，你打算下什么决心？
How are you going to keep them?
你打算怎样坚持它们？

五建议学习顺序

  1. 先听全文音频 1—2 遍，不看书，抓大意（谁、做了什么、为什么）。
  2. 切到“逐句对照”，点每句左边的 ▶ 一句一句跟读；橙黄色的是生词，点一下看音标和例句，再点一下关掉。
  3. 句子下方的浅绿注解是重点短语：释义、例句、译文都在里面，点小 ▶ 可以单独听。
  4. 默认只显示英文，最适合跟读；想看中文，就打开右上“显示译文与注解”（一键全开），或者点某句右边的“译”只放这一行。
  5. 只看中文译文，尝试口头把每一段“译回”英文，再与原文比对。
  6. 做题，用“依据句”核对答案出处；错题标出是细节题、猜词题还是主旨题。
  7. 睡前再听一遍全文，不看文字，检验理解是否顺。
