The Secret of a Shorter Subway Transfer

地铁换乘怎么少走路

原文 264 词 5 段 · 23 句 27 个生词 17 个短语 5 道题

讲大城市地铁换乘靠颜色、标识、箭头和电梯的“智能设计”让人少走冤枉路、坐对方向。全文 23 句、5 段，实用说明文，句间逻辑靠 first / next / during / before 串起来，适合练“按顺序找信息”。原卷未给答案，本页答案为依据原文推断，仅供参考。

语速

一原文先通读一遍，再做题

In many big cities, the subway is the fastest way to travel. However, changing lines can be confusing for new riders. Today, city planners use smart design to help people find their way easily.

When you enter a busy interchange station, look at the colors first. Each subway line has its own special color on the walls and maps. This clear signage helps you tell different lines apart quickly. Following the right color saves you time before you even walk far.

Next, you need to find the correct platform for your train. Do not just look at the platform number alone. You must check both the number and the direction together carefully. Trains on the same platform often go in opposite directions. Good navigation means reading every detail on the boards above you. Arrows will point you to the right path safely.

During a transfer, always follow the colored arrows on the floor or walls. They guide you step by step to the next line without wrong turns. But remember, the shortest stairs are not always the best choice for everyone. If you push a wheelchair or a baby car, stairs are hard to use. Modern stations focus heavily on accessibility for all kinds of passengers. People with special needs might have to take a longer but smoother route. Elevators are usually placed along these specific accessible paths.

Before you leave the transfer hall, stop to check your route one last time. Make sure the line color matches your final destination again. Smart design makes our daily travel much easier and friendlier than before.

二阅读理解题先自己做，再展开答案

1.What should you look at first when you enter a busy interchange station?

走进繁忙的换乘站时，你应该先看什么？

  • A.The platform numbers（站台号）
  • B.Colors on the walls and maps（墙面和地图上的颜色）
  • C.The arrows on the floor（地面上的箭头）
  • D.The elevators in the hall（大厅里的电梯）
看答案与依据

答案：B

依据句：When you enter a busy interchange station, look at the colors first.

解析：细节题：第二段第一句就用 look at the colors first 直接给了顺序，first 是关键词。

2.Why might some people take a longer route during a transfer?

换乘时，为什么有些人会走一条更长的路线？

  • A.Because they want to see more of the station（因为他们想多看看车站）
  • B.Because the shorter stairs are closed for repair（因为较短的楼梯在维修、封了）
  • C.Because they need to use elevators instead of stairs（因为他们需要坐电梯代替走楼梯）
  • D.Because they like walking slowly with friends（因为他们喜欢和朋友慢慢走）
看答案与依据

答案：C

依据句：If you push a wheelchair or a baby car, stairs are hard to use. … People with special needs might have to take a longer but smoother route. Elevators are usually placed along these specific accessible paths.

解析：因果题：楼梯用不了（stairs are hard to use）→ 走更长更平顺的路（longer but smoother route）→ 因为要坐电梯（elevators）。

3.How can you make sure you get on the right train?

怎样才能确保坐上正确的列车？

  • A.Only read the platform number quickly（只快速看一眼站台号）
  • B.Ask other passengers which way to go（问其他乘客往哪走）
  • C.Take the shortest stairs you can find（走你能找到的最短的楼梯）
  • D.Check the color and direction, and follow the arrows to the right platform（核对颜色和方向，跟着箭头走到正确的站台）
看答案与依据

答案：D

依据句：You must check both the number and the direction together carefully. … Arrows will point you to the right path safely.

解析：细节题：第三段给出两件事——number 与 direction 一起核对，再跟着 arrows 走。选项 A 正是原文否定的做法（Do not just look at the platform number alone）。

4.What is the main idea of this passage?

这篇文章的主旨是什么？

  • A.Subway trains are faster than buses in big cities（在大城市，地铁比公交快）
  • B.How smart design helps passengers change subway lines easily（智能设计如何帮助乘客轻松换乘地铁）
  • C.Why new riders should not take the subway alone（为什么新乘客不该独自坐地铁）
  • D.Where to find elevators in a modern subway station（在现代地铁站里哪里能找到电梯）
看答案与依据

答案：B

依据句：Today, city planners use smart design to help people find their way easily. … Smart design makes our daily travel much easier and friendlier than before.

解析：主旨题：首段的 smart design + help people find their way 与末段的 smart design + easier 首尾呼应；A、C、D 都只是文中一句话的局部信息。

5.In the last paragraph, what does the word 'destination' mean?

最后一段中 destination 一词是什么意思？

  • A.The ticket you buy at the station（你在车站买的票）
  • B.The train you wait for on the platform（你在站台等的列车）
  • C.The place you want to go to（你要去的地方）
  • D.The time your train leaves（列车开出的时间）
看答案与依据

答案：C

依据句：Before you leave the transfer hall, stop to check your route one last time. Make sure the line color matches your final destination again.

解析：猜词题：destination 与 route（路线）搭配，指“要去的终点”；de- 表指向、-ation 是名词后缀，字面就是“被指定的地方”。

第 1 段
In many big cities, the subway is the fastest way to travel.
在许多大城市，地铁是最快的出行方式。
the fastest way to travel 最快的出行方式 · The subway is the fastest way to travel in big cities. （在大城市，地铁是最快的出行方式。）
However, changing lines can be confusing for new riders.
不过，换乘线路对新手乘客来说很容易搞混。
change lines 换乘线路 · You need to change lines at the next station. （你需要在下一站换乘。）
be confusing for sb 让某人感到困惑 · The map is confusing for new riders. （这张地图让新乘客很困惑。）
Today, city planners use smart design to help people find their way easily.
如今，城市规划者用智能设计帮助人们轻松找到路。
find one's way 找到路，认得路 · She could not find her way in the big station. （她在这个大车站里找不到路。）
第 2 段
When you enter a busy interchange station, look at the colors first.
走进一座繁忙的换乘站时，先看颜色。
Each subway line has its own special color on the walls and maps.
每条地铁线路在墙面和地图上都有自己专属的颜色。
This clear signage helps you tell different lines apart quickly.
这种清晰的标识能帮你迅速区分不同的线路。
tell A apart 把……区分开 · Can you tell the two lines apart? （你能分清这两条线路吗？）
Following the right color saves you time before you even walk far.
只要跟着正确的颜色走，还没走远就已经省下了时间。
save sb time 为某人省下时间 · Taking the right exit saves you time. （走对出口能为你省时间。）
第 3 段
Next, you need to find the correct platform for your train.
接下来，你要找到自己那趟车所在的站台。
Do not just look at the platform number alone.
不要只看站台号。
You must check both the number and the direction together carefully.
你必须把站台号和方向放在一起仔细核对。
Trains on the same platform often go in opposite directions.
同一个站台两边的列车，开往的方向常常是相反的。
go in opposite directions 朝相反的方向行驶 · The two trains go in opposite directions. （这两列车朝相反的方向行驶。）
Good navigation means reading every detail on the boards above you.
良好的“导航”，意味着把头顶指示牌上的每个细节都读清楚。
Arrows will point you to the right path safely.
箭头会安全地把你引向正确的通道。
point sb to 把某人引向，给某人指路 · The sign points you to the exit. （这个标志把你引向出口。）
第 4 段
During a transfer, always follow the colored arrows on the floor or walls.
换乘途中，始终跟着地面或墙上的彩色箭头走。
They guide you step by step to the next line without wrong turns.
它们会一步一步把你带到下一条线，不会走错弯。
step by step 一步一步地 · Follow the arrows step by step. （一步一步跟着箭头走。）
without wrong turns 不走错路 · He found the platform without wrong turns. （他没走错路就找到了站台。）
But remember, the shortest stairs are not always the best choice for everyone.
但要记住，最短的楼梯对每个人来说不一定是最好的选择。
If you push a wheelchair or a baby car, stairs are hard to use.
如果你推着轮椅或婴儿车，楼梯就很难用。
be hard to use 很难使用 · These old stairs are hard to use. （这些旧楼梯很难走。）
Modern stations focus heavily on accessibility for all kinds of passengers.
现代车站非常注重无障碍设计，照顾各类乘客。
focus on 专注于，着重于 · The station focuses on safety. （这个车站把重点放在安全上。）
all kinds of 各种各样的 · The shop sells all kinds of tickets. （这家店卖各种各样的票。）
People with special needs might have to take a longer but smoother route.
有特殊需要的人可能不得不走一条更长但更平顺的路线。
have to do sth 不得不做某事 · You have to show your ticket here. （你必须在这里出示车票。）
Elevators are usually placed along these specific accessible paths.
电梯通常就设在这些专门的无障碍通道旁。
第 5 段
Before you leave the transfer hall, stop to check your route one last time.
离开换乘大厅之前，停下来把路线最后核对一遍。
one last time 最后再……一次 · Check your bag one last time. （最后再检查一次你的包。）
Make sure the line color matches your final destination again.
再次确认线路颜色和你最终要去的地方一致。
make sure 确保，核实 · Make sure the door is closed. （确认门关好了。）
Smart design makes our daily travel much easier and friendlier than before.
智能设计让我们的日常出行比过去轻松、友好得多。
daily travel 日常出行 · The new line makes daily travel easier. （这条新线路让日常出行更方便。）

五建议学习顺序

  1. 先听全文音频 1—2 遍，不看书，抓大意（谁、做了什么、为什么）。
  2. 切到“逐句对照”，一句一句跟读；橙黄色的是生词，点一下看音标和例句，再点一下关掉。
  3. 句子下方的浅绿注解是重点短语：释义、例句、译文都在里面，点小 ▶ 可以单独听。
  4. 只想练跟读时，把“显示短语注解”关掉，页面就只剩原文和译文。
  5. 只看中文译文，尝试口头把每一段“译回”英文，再与原文比对。
  6. 做题，用“依据句”核对答案出处；错题标出是细节题、猜词题还是主旨题。
  7. 睡前再听一遍全文，不看文字，检验理解是否顺。
